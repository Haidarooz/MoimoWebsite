Moimo

Your life, on a single timeline

Moimo is a visual timeline of everything that matters to you — past, present, and future. Plot milestones, track habits, plan goals, and preserve memories on a single, zoomable canvas anchored to your life.

Download on the App Store

Key Features

Talk to Your Timeline

Type or speak naturally to add, edit, move, or reorganize events. Say "add a gym session every Monday starting next week" or "push my vacation back two weeks" — Moimo proposes the changes, you review and confirm.

AI Overview

Now — what needs your attention today. Enhancements — ways to improve, combine, or clean up existing events. Suggestions — new events the AI recommends based on your patterns.

Multiple Timelines

Organize your life by domain — Work, Personal, Health, or anything you choose. Each timeline is its own colored lane on the canvas.

Track Progress

Turn any event into a task with checklists. Track completion at a glance — color-coded status badges show what's done, in progress, or overdue right on the timeline.

Recurring Events

Set daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or custom intervals. Past occurrences appear as a faded visual history, so you can see your streaks and patterns over time.

Rich Event Details

Attach photos, record voice notes, pin locations, and write descriptions. Every event can be as simple or as detailed as you want.

Discover Blueprints

Browse community blueprints — AI-curated timeline templates for fitness plans, career roadmaps, creative projects, and more. Pick a start date and adopt one as your own.

Share & Collaborate

Share a snapshot of any timeline as a read-only link others can adopt. Invite others to co-edit a timeline in real time with Moimo+.

Focus Now Widget

A home screen widget showing AI-prioritized items that need your attention, updated throughout the day.

Calendar Import

Bring in events from your existing iOS calendars. Select a date range, pick which calendars to include, and import in one tap.
